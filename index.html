§ II.

Method & Pipeline

Step 01

Preprocess

Lowercase the text, strip punctuation and stopwords, then lemmatize. Articles from True.csv are labeled 1; articles from Fake.csv are labeled 0. The two are concatenated and shuffled.

Step 02

Vectorize

Apply TfidfVectorizer with unigrams and bigrams. Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency converts each article into a sparse, weighted vector where rare-but-informative terms outrank common filler.

Step 03

Classify

Train both MultinomialNB (probabilistic baseline) and LinearSVC (max-margin separator) on an 80/20 split. Evaluate via accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and a confusion matrix.

Verdict.  The Linear SVM separates the two classes with near-perfect accuracy on the held-out test set. Naïve Bayes serves as a fast probabilistic baseline — adequate, but outclassed when the feature space is high-dimensional and the boundary is approximately linear.
